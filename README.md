# Customer_Segmentation
                                Customer Segmentation and Business Analytics
Objective:
The objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behavior, segment customers into meaningful groups, and predict customer spending categories using machine learning techniques. The goal is to help businesses improve marketing strategies and customer engagement through data-driven insights.
Problem Statement:
Businesses often collect large amounts of customer data but fail to utilize it effectively for decision-making. Without customer segmentation, marketing campaigns become less efficient and less personalized.
This project focuses on:
•	Identifying distinct customer segments based on income and spending patterns.
•	Predicting whether a customer is a Low, Medium, or High spender.
•	Generating actionable business insights for targeted marketing and customer retention.
